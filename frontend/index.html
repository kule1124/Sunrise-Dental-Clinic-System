Help

  • 1. How to register an appointment: use the register appointment form and save patient details.
  • 2. How to search an appointment: enter the appointment number in Search Appointment.
  • 3. How to view patient records: open Patient Records and search by patient name or appointment number.
  • 4. How to calculate a bill: use Billing and enter the appointment number to generate the total.
  • 5. How to print a receipt: generate the bill and then print the receipt from the billing section.
  • 6. How to logout: click the Logout button in the left sidebar or the red exit action.
